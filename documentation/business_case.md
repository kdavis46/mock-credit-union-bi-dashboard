# Business Case

## Problem Statement

Georgia United Credit Union lacked centralized visibility across core operational domains including members, accounts, transactions, and loans.

Leadership could not easily answer key business questions related to:
- growth trends
- transaction activity
- deposit concentration
- loan exposure
- delinquency risk

## Objective

Develop a centralized Business Intelligence dashboard capable of delivering executive-level KPI reporting and operational insights through a single reporting solution.

## Solution

A multi-page Power BI dashboard was developed using PostgreSQL, SQL, and DAX to centralize reporting across four interconnected business domains:
- Members
- Accounts
- Transactions
- Loans

## Business Value

The dashboard enables leadership to:
- monitor organizational performance
- identify operational risks
- analyze member behavior
- evaluate loan portfolio health
- support data-driven decision-making
