# Findings & Recommendations

## Key Findings

### Loan Delinquency Risk
The dashboard identified 232 overdue loans across 747 total loans, representing approximately 31% delinquency exposure.

### Declining Member Growth
Membership growth trends showed visible decline beginning in later reporting years.

### Account Concentration Risk
A small group of high-value accounts represented a disproportionate share of total deposits.

---

# Recommendations

## Improve Delinquency Monitoring
Implement segmented delinquency monitoring and outreach processes focused on high-risk loan categories.

## Increase Member Retention
Develop re-engagement campaigns targeting inactive or low-engagement members.

## Monitor High-Value Accounts
Establish relationship management processes for high-balance account holders to reduce deposit concentration risk.
